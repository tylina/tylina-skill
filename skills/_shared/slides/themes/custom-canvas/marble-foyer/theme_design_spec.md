## III. Visual Theme

### Design Philosophy

Luxury marble and architectural foyer aesthetic. Think grand hotel lobby, museum entrance, polished stone surfaces with gold trim. Pale marble-white backgrounds with subtle veining pattern SVGs, gold metallic accents, and classical architectural elements (columns, arches, cornices, keystones). Every slide feels like standing in a grand marble foyer -- sophisticated, weighty, luxurious.

## IV. SVG Definitions (5 intricate SVGs)

### 1. Marble Vein Texture (Full-page)
- **Purpose**: Persistent atmosphere on all content slides
- **Elements**: 12 organic curved paths simulating natural stone veining
- **Opacity range**: 0.04 to 0.12
- **Stroke widths**: 0.3pt to 1.5pt
- **ViewBox**: 800x450 (full slide proportion)

### 2. Classical Column Border
- **Purpose**: Vertical framing elements for title/ending slides
- **Elements**: 15+ elements including fluted shaft lines, base moldings, capital volutes
- **Opacity range**: 0.06 to 0.35
- **ViewBox**: 60x400

### 3. Cornice Molding Header
- **Purpose**: Horizontal header decoration with dentil pattern
- **Elements**: 50+ elements (dentil blocks, molding layers, ogee curve)
- **Opacity range**: 0.06 to 0.30
- **ViewBox**: 800x50

### 4. Arch Keystone Ornament
- **Purpose**: Focal element for section transitions and decorative accents
- **Elements**: 12 elements including arch curves, keystone block, voussoirs, center ornament
- **Opacity range**: 0.10 to 0.40
- **ViewBox**: 200x120

### 5. Gold Leaf Corner Piece
- **Purpose**: Ornate corner brackets for frames and cards
- **Elements**: 13 elements including acanthus scrolls, filigree curves, circle accents
- **Opacity range**: 0.15 to 0.40
- **ViewBox**: 80x80

## V. Slide Types

### title-slide
Full architectural frame with columns on both sides, cornice at top, gold corner pieces at all four corners, inner gold double-frame, arch keystone above title, marble veining background. Centered content: institution kicker, title, gold divider with center dot, subtitle, author/date.

### new-section-slide
Marble veining, cornice at top, column accent on left side, large arch keystone (top-right), ghost section number (72pt, 82% transparent), "SECTION XX" monospace label, bold section title, plinth-line accent.

### slide (standard content)
Persistent marble atmosphere: full-page veining, faint cornice at top, gold corner pieces at bottom corners. Header: bold title + gold double plinth-line. Footer: configured footer content (or monospace "MARBLE FOYER") | gold diamond | page counter.

### dark-slide
Onyx/charcoal background. Light marble veining (85% transparent), arch keystone background element, gold corner accents. Light header/footer. Text defaults to marble-white.

### focus-slide
Marble background with enhanced veining, arch keystone centered above, gold corner pieces at all four corners, corner accent lines. Centered italic statement text.

### ending-slide
Bookends with title-slide: identical architectural frame (columns + cornice + gold corners + gold frames), ghost "FOYER" text, arch keystone ornament, centered farewell with gold divider, "MARBLE FOYER" mark.

## VII. Persistent Atmosphere

Every standard content slide receives the `_marble-atmosphere` function which places:
1. Full-page marble veining SVG (secondary color, 30% transparent)
2. Faint cornice molding at top edge (88% transparent)
3. Gold corner pieces at bottom-left and bottom-right (85% transparent, rotated)

## VIII. Bookending Strategy

The title-slide and ending-slide share identical architectural framing:
- Full-page marble veining
- Cornice molding at top
- Column borders on left and right
- Gold corner pieces at all four corners (same size, same opacity)
- Inner double gold frame (82%/80% width, 78%/76% height)

This creates visual bookending that gives the presentation a sense of architectural completeness.
