## III. Visual Theme

### Design Philosophy

**A Carnival parade made of light.** This theme captures the explosive energy, saturated color, and joyful exuberance of Brazilian Carnival. Every slide lives in a world of confetti, feathers, and golden sequins. The warm white background evokes daytime parades under tropical sun, while vivid accent colors burst across the canvas like samba dancers.

The theme is the OPPOSITE of minimalism -- it is maximalist, joyful, and celebratory. Yet it maintains readability through careful layering (background decorations at high transparency, clear card-based content areas with opaque fills).

## V. Slide Type Definitions

### Cover Slide (`title-slide`)

- **Background**: Warm white (#FFFCF5) with MAXIMALIST decoration
- **Layout**: Center-aligned title card with drop shadow, surrounded by explosion of decorative elements
- **Decorative elements**: 3 layers of confetti scatter, feather plumes (left, right, bottom-left), tropical leaves (corners), sequin sparkles (scattered), rhythm waves (bottom)
- **Card**: White with gradient stripe (pink-gold-green) at top, rhythm wave divider
- **Colors**: Dark text on white card, vivid decorations surrounding

### Section Slide (`new-section-slide`)

- **Background**: Warm white with confetti and feather plume
- **Layout**: Section number in circle badge (top-right), title bottom-left with rhythm wave underline
- **Decorative elements**: Confetti scatter, feather plume (right), tropical leaf (bottom-left), sequin sparkle next to number badge
- **Numbering**: Circle badge with `utils.display-current-heading-number(level: 1, numbering: "01")`

### Content Slide (`slide`)

- **Background**: Warm white (#FFFCF5) with persistent atmosphere
- **Header**: Bold title + rhythm wave underline (NOT a straight line)
- **Footer**: Three-column grid: "CARNIVAL RIO" | floral dingbat | page counter
- **Persistent atmosphere**: Confetti scatter (2 layers), tropical leaf (bottom-right corner), rhythm wave (bottom edge)

### Dark Slide (`dark-slide`)

- **Background**: Deep purple-black (#1A0A1E)
- **Text colors**: Warm white (#FFF8F0)
- **Decorative elements**: Ghost feather plume (right), faint confetti, sequin sparkles
- **Header/footer**: Inverted chrome with muted text
- **Parameters**: `title`, `header-left`, `header-right`

### Focus Slide (`focus-slide`)

- **Background**: Hot carnival pink (#FF2D55)
- **Text**: White, large, bold, centered
- **Decorative elements**: White confetti, feather plumes (corners), sequin sparkles
- **Width-constrained**: Content in block to prevent overflow

### Ending Slide (`ending-slide`)

- **Background**: Warm white with FULL carnival decoration (bookends with title-slide)
- **Shared elements with title**: Confetti layers, feather plumes, tropical leaves, sequin sparkles, rhythm waves
- **Center card**: White with gradient stripe, sequin sparkle ornament, rhythm wave divider
- **Ghost text**: "SAMBA" in very low opacity

## VII. Design Principles

| # | Principle | Rationale |
|---|-----------|-----------|
| 1 | Maximalism over minimalism | Carnival IS excess -- every slide should feel alive with color and movement |
| 2 | Warm white base, vivid accents | Light background keeps content readable; color bursts from decorative elements |
| 3 | Three-color harmony (pink/gold/green) | Brazilian flag + carnival costume palette -- always harmonious, never muddy |
| 4 | Persistent world-building | Even the simplest content slide has confetti and tropical elements -- you never leave the carnival |
| 5 | Bookending through decoration | Title and ending slides share the same explosive decoration, creating narrative arc |
