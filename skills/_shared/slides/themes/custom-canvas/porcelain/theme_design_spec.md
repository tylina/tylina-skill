## III. Visual Theme

### Design Philosophy

Ming dynasty Jingdezhen blue-and-white porcelain aesthetic. Every slide feels like the surface of a porcelain vessel: white glaze backgrounds with cobalt blue decorative motifs painted on top. Lattice borders simulate the rim of a plate, cloud scrolls and peonies provide organic decoration, wave patterns mark transitions, and a dragon motif dominates dark (cobalt) slides. Gold accents represent the gilded rim of imperial ware.

## V. Slide Type Definitions

### Cover Slide (`title-slide`)

- **Background**: Clean white glaze (`pal.glaze`)
- **Layout**: Centered content with generous padding (6em x, 3em y)
- **Decorative elements**: Full-page lattice frame (78% transparent), large peony flowers (160pt top-right, 110pt bottom-left), wave border along bottom, cloud scrolls (top-left, bottom-right), double gold rim frame (92%/90% width inner rectangles), gold dot accents at all frame corners
- **Colors**: Institution kicker in tiny monospace (ink-muted), title at display size in ink, subtitle in italic ink-dim, author/date in tiny monospace meta-color

### Section Slide (`new-section-slide`)

- **Background**: White glaze with lattice frame (85% transparent)
- **Layout**: Left-aligned content (4em left, 10em right, 7em top, 3em bottom padding)
- **Decorative elements**: Large peony (180pt, top-right at 45% transparent), cloud scroll (100pt, bottom-left), large ghost section number (72pt, 80% transparent in top-left), accent line with gold dot
- **Numbering**: "SECTION XX" monospace label + bold section title + rim accent below + wave border at very bottom
- **Footer**: "PORCELAIN" monospace mark at bottom-right

### Content Slide (`slide`)

- **Background**: White glaze with persistent porcelain atmosphere (lattice frame at 88% transparent, cloud scrolls in two corners at 92% transparent, ghost character at 97% transparent)
- **Header**: Bold ink title (1.4em) with double-line rim accent below (1.2pt + 0.5pt offset)
- **Footer**: Three-column: "PORCELAIN" monospace | gold diamond | page number
- **Content area**: Open area atop the porcelain atmosphere

### Dark Slide (`dark-slide`)

- **Background**: Cobalt blue (`pal.cobalt`)
- **Layout**: Standard margins (top: 2.8em, bottom: 1.2em, x: 2.5em) with custom header/footer
- **Decorative elements**: Dragon motif SVG dominating center (380pt wide, 75% transparent gold), cloud scroll accents (cream, 88% transparent) in top-left and bottom-right, ghost character in cream (95% transparent)
- **Header**: Custom left/right content in small cream monospace
- **Footer**: "PORCELAIN" | gold diamond | page number in cream
- **Text colors**: Cream white (`#F0EBE0`) for all text

### Focus Slide (`focus-slide`)

- **Background**: Warm glaze (`pal.glaze-warm`) with 3em margins
- **Layout**: Centered, horizontal + vertical
- **Decorative elements**: Lattice frame at 90% transparent (oversized 110%), cloud scrolls in all 4 corners (larger pair + smaller pair), gold dot accents (2 corners), ghost character at 96% transparent
- **Text**: 1.4em italic ink text, centered

### Ending Slide (`ending-slide`)

- **Background**: White glaze, bookends with title (same lattice frame, peonies mirrored, cloud scrolls, gold rim frame, wave border)
- **Layout**: Centered with 5em x padding
- **Decorative elements**: Seal mark ornament, decorative gold-dot divider line, "PORCELAIN" monospace label
- **Text**: 2em bold ink, seal mark above, divider below

## VII. Design Principles

| # | Principle | Rationale |
|---|-----------|-----------|
| 1 | Porcelain surface metaphor | Every light slide = the glazed surface of a vessel |
| 2 | Blue and gold only (per preset) | Constraint mirrors historical craft -- cobalt blue + gilded rim |
| 3 | Persistent lattice frame | Every slide has the hui-wen border visible like a plate rim |
| 4 | Organic SVG decorations | Cloud scrolls, peonies, waves -- hand-painted feeling |
| 5 | Dark slides = the cobalt body | Dark-slide uses cobalt blue (the ceramic body before glazing) with dragon motif |
