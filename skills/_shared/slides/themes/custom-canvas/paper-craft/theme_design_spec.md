## III. Visual Theme

### Design Philosophy

A craft table where someone is making a beautiful paper collage. Colored paper layers overlap with visible drop shadows creating physical dimensionality. Torn edges reveal layers beneath. Paper clips hold elements to the page. Scissors marks serve as dividers. The design is tactile, joyful, and DIMENSIONAL -- every element looks like a separate paper layer physically stacked.

## V. Slide Type Definitions

### Title Slide (`title-slide`)

- **Background**: Warm cream with paper texture, craft cutting-mat grid at 3%
- **Decorative elements**: Origami crane hero (top-right), second smaller crane (bottom-left), paper clips, layered colored paper rectangles with shadows (3 stacks at different rotations), torn paper strips across top/bottom
- **Central card**: Drop shadow, torn edge accent top, institution kicker, title, scissors divider, subtitle, author + date

### Section Slide (`new-section-slide`)

- **Background**: Paper texture, torn edge across middle revealing colored layer
- **Section badge**: Circular cutout with shadow (top-right), paper clip attached
- **Content**: Section number kicker, heading text, torn edge underline
- **Decorative**: Small origami crane (bottom-right)

### Content Slide (`slide`)

- **Background atmosphere (persistent)**: Paper fiber texture, fold crease in top-right at 8%, paper clip holding header, torn edge at bottom at 6%
- **Header**: Bold title with TORN EDGE underline (not straight line)
- **Footer**: "PAPER-CRAFT" (left), scissors Unicode (center), page number (right)
- **Body**: Horizonally aligned content area

### Dark Slide (`dark-slide`)

- **Background**: Deep navy `#1A1A2E`
- **Ghost**: Large origami crane outline at 5%
- **Texture**: Paper texture at 4%, scattered lighter paper fragments
- **Header**: Footer text + page counter (muted)
- **Footer**: Same structure as content slide

### Focus Slide (`focus-slide`)

- **Background**: Accent color (coral)
- **Decorative**: Paper texture at 6%, torn edges top/bottom at 25%, scattered paper fragments, paper clip
- **Content**: Large bold white text centered

### Ending Slide (`ending-slide`)

- **Background**: Mirrors title-slide (bookending) -- same cream, craft grid, torn strips, origami cranes
- **Ghost text**: "CRAFT" at 92% transparent
- **Central card**: Shadow, torn edge top, origami crane ornament, text, scissors divider, "PAPER-CRAFT" label

## VII. Design Principles

| # | Principle | Rationale |
|---|-----------|-----------|
| 1 | DROP SHADOWS on everything | Every card uses offset colored rectangle behind to create physical depth |
| 2 | Torn edges, never straight lines | Header underlines, card borders, page decorations use irregular torn-edge SVG |
| 3 | Warm cream background, never pure white | The page IS paper -- `#FFFDF8` simulates real paper |
| 4 | Persistent world-building | Every content slide has atmosphere: texture, fold crease, clip, bottom torn edge |
| 5 | Bookending title/ending | Same decorative vocabulary (cranes, clips, paper stacks) for visual unity |

## VIII. SVG Decorations (7 Custom)

| SVG | Elements | Usage |
|-----|----------|-------|
| Torn paper edge | 40+ path segments (irregular wobble) | Card top borders, slide decorations, header underlines |
| Paper clip | Double-curve bent wire | "Holds" elements to page, header attachment |
| Scissors + cut line | Open scissors + dashed extending line | Divider component, title card decoration |
| Paper fold crease | Diagonal fold + triangular shadow | Background atmosphere (corner fold) |
| Origami crane | 13 elements (outline + internal fold lines) | Title/ending hero, dark-slide ghost, section decoration |
| Paper texture | 24 fine fiber/grain lines | Persistent background on all slides |
| Washi tape | Patterned semi-transparent strip | tape-strip component decoration |

## IX. Drop Shadow System (KEY)

```typst
// Behind every card: an offset colored rectangle
place(dx: 4pt, dy: 4pt,
  block(width: 100%, height: 100%, fill: rgb("#00000018"), radius: 3pt))
// Then the actual card on top
block(fill: palette.paper-1, radius: 3pt, ...)
```

This creates the LAYERED PAPER effect. Without shadows, it is flat rectangles. With shadows, it is PAPER CRAFT.
