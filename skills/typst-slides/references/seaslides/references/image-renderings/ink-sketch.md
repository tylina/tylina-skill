# Style Anchor: ink-sketch

Hand-drawn pen-on-paper aesthetic with visible stroke variation, paper texture, and a deliberately imperfect linework quality. Primarily monochrome with one strategic accent color.

---

## 1. Visual Characteristics

- Visible pen strokes with natural thickness variation (0.5pt to 3pt)
- Warm off-white or cream paper texture as base surface
- Crosshatch shading for depth rather than filled tones
- Monochrome ink (black or dark sepia) plus one accent color only
- Intentional imperfection — slightly uneven lines, organic curves

---

## 2. Best-For Scenarios

- Creative agency and design portfolio decks
- Academic lectures and research presentations
- Notebook-style workshop and brainstorm recaps
- Methodology and framework explanations
- Any deck where a personal, hand-crafted feel builds connection

---

## 3. Prompt Fragment (paste as opening sentences)

> Hand-drawn ink sketch on warm cream paper, visible pen strokes with natural line-weight variation from 0.5pt to 3pt. Crosshatch shading, no solid fills. Primarily black ink with one single accent color for emphasis. Paper texture visible throughout as a subtle grain.

---

## 4. Color Behavior

- Primary HEX becomes the single accent color (used sparingly — circles, underlines, one highlighted element)
- The ink itself is always near-black or dark sepia regardless of palette
- Secondary HEX may tint the paper background very subtly (5-10% wash)
- Accent is the primary; there is no separate "accent" — the monochrome ink does the heavy structural work

---

## 5. Don'ts

- No solid color fills (ink sketch relies on hatching and line density)
- No more than one accent hue — two accent colors destroys the monochrome discipline
- No digital-perfect straight lines or bezier curves (must feel hand-drawn)
- No photorealistic rendering or smooth shading
- No heavy saturated backgrounds (the paper must breathe)

---

## 6. Example Prompt

> Hand-drawn ink sketch on warm cream paper, visible pen strokes with natural line-weight variation from 0.5pt to 3pt. Crosshatch shading, no solid fills. Primarily black ink with one single accent color for emphasis. Paper texture visible throughout as a subtle grain. A notebook-style diagram showing three connected idea clusters — each cluster is a loose hand-drawn circle containing small symbolic icons (a lightbulb, a gear, a target), connected by flowing arrow lines with slight wobble. The clusters are arranged in a triangular composition occupying the central 70% of the frame. Crosshatch density increases toward the cluster centers to create focal weight. One cluster is circled with a confident stroke of teal ink (#0D9488) as the sole accent — everything else is black ink on cream. Aspect ratio 4:3, landscape. No text, no written words, no labels. The cream paper (#FDF6E3) shows subtle fiber texture throughout.
