# Style Anchor: flat-vector

Geometric illustration with sharp edges, limited palette (3-4 colors), no shadows, no depth simulation. Shapes are confident and deliberate — circles, rectangles, rounded forms — each filled with a single solid color.

---

## 1. Visual Characteristics

- Flat solid fills only — no gradients, no shadows, no blur
- Sharp geometric edges with uniform stroke weight (0pt or 2pt max)
- Limited to 3-4 distinct colors per composition
- Generous negative space (30-50% of canvas is breathing room)
- Simplified forms reduced to essential silhouettes

---

## 2. Best-For Scenarios

- Technology explainer decks and product walkthroughs
- SaaS and startup pitch presentations
- Training and onboarding materials
- Process and workflow illustrations
- Any deck where clarity and scalability matter more than atmosphere

---

## 3. Prompt Fragment (paste as opening sentences)

> Flat vector illustration with sharp geometric shapes, solid color fills, no shadows, no depth. Limited to 3-4 colors with crisp edges on a neutral background. Forms are simplified to essential silhouettes — every element reads clearly at any scale.

---

## 4. Color Behavior

- Primary HEX fills the dominant subject shape (the largest filled form)
- Secondary HEX occupies the background field or large negative-space block
- Accent HEX is a single small element — a dot, arrow, or highlight — totaling under 10% of area
- No blending between colors; each region is a distinct flat zone

---

## 5. Don'ts

- No shadows, drop shadows, or ambient occlusion of any kind
- No gradients or color transitions within a single shape
- No texture, grain, or paper simulation
- No outlines thicker than 2pt (or they dominate the minimal aesthetic)
- No more than 4 distinct hues — adding a 5th breaks the controlled palette

---
