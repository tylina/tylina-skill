# Style Anchor: collage-editorial

Mixed-media composition with cut-paper layers, magazine clippings, overlapping textures, and deliberate visual friction. Elements look physically assembled rather than digitally composed.

---

## 1. Visual Characteristics

- Visible cut edges — torn paper, scissor-cut lines, ragged borders
- Overlapping layers with subtle paper-thickness shadows between them
- Textural variety: newsprint, colored stock, kraft paper, tissue
- Intentional misalignment and rotation — nothing sits perfectly square
- Mixed scale: one large anchor element surrounded by smaller fragments

---

## 2. Best-For Scenarios

- Fashion and lifestyle editorial presentations
- Cultural commentary and magazine-style decks
- Experimental brand and creative agency work
- Music, film, and media industry pitches
- Any deck where visual surprise and textural richness are valued

---

## 3. Prompt Fragment (paste as opening sentences)

> Mixed-media collage with cut-paper layers and overlapping textures. Visible scissor-cut and torn edges, subtle paper-thickness shadows between layers. Elements appear physically assembled — slightly rotated, intentionally misaligned, with textural variety across newsprint, colored stock, and kraft paper surfaces.

---

## 4. Color Behavior

- Primary HEX appears as the largest colored-paper layer (a dominant background sheet)
- Secondary HEX manifests as a contrasting paper stock layer or the exposed base surface
- Accent HEX appears in one small clipped element — a torn strip, a circular cutout, a stamp-like mark
- Colors feel tactile and slightly muted by paper surface texture (not screen-bright)

---

## 5. Don'ts

- No digitally smooth blending between layers (each layer must have a distinct edge)
- No perfect geometric alignment (the style depends on slight rotation and offset)
- No photorealistic photography filling the entire frame (photos appear as clipped fragments only)
- No more than 4-5 distinct layers (too many creates visual noise rather than composed tension)
- No clean white digital backgrounds — the base surface should have texture (kraft, newsprint, canvas)

---
