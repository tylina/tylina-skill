# Style Anchor: abstract-geometric

Pure geometric shapes in bold saturated colors, Bauhaus-inspired compositions with asymmetric balance. No representational subjects — meaning is conveyed through form, color weight, and spatial tension.

---

## 1. Visual Characteristics

- Pure geometry only: circles, triangles, rectangles, arcs, lines
- Bold saturated color at full chroma — no pastels, no muted tones
- Asymmetric composition with deliberate visual tension and counterbalance
- Hard edges, no feathering, no blur between color zones
- Strong figure-ground contrast with decisive use of scale difference

---

## 2. Best-For Scenarios

- Brand identity and visual system launches
- Festival and event promotional decks
- Editorial covers and section dividers
- Bold campaign and manifesto presentations
- Any deck where visual confidence matters more than literal depiction

---

## 3. Prompt Fragment (paste as opening sentences)

> Abstract geometric composition inspired by Bauhaus and De Stijl — pure shapes only, no representational subjects. Bold saturated colors at full chroma with hard edges, asymmetric balance, and deliberate scale contrast between large and small forms.

---

## 4. Color Behavior

- Primary HEX fills the single largest geometric form (the dominant visual anchor)
- Secondary HEX fills the background field or the second-largest shape
- Accent HEX is a small but intense element — a circle, a line, a triangle — creating focal pull
- Colors are used at near-full saturation; the style demands chromatic confidence

---

## 5. Don'ts

- No representational imagery (no people, objects, scenes, icons)
- No soft edges, feathering, or gaussian blur between shapes
- No more than 4-5 distinct shapes per composition (complexity kills the boldness)
- No pastel or desaturated colors (the style requires full-chroma commitment)
- No symmetrical centering — the composition must feel dynamically balanced, not static

---

## 6. Example Prompt

> Abstract geometric composition inspired by Bauhaus and De Stijl — pure shapes only, no representational subjects. Bold saturated colors at full chroma with hard edges, asymmetric balance, and deliberate scale contrast between large and small forms. A large circle (60% of canvas height) anchors the lower-left quadrant, partially cropped by the frame edge. A thin horizontal bar crosses the upper-third of the canvas at full width. Three small squares of descending size stack vertically near the right edge, creating rhythmic counterpoint to the dominant circle. The circle is deep crimson (#991B1B) at full saturation, the horizontal bar is midnight navy (#1E1B4B), the small squares are warm gold (#CA8A04), and the background field is stark ivory (#FFFFF0). Aspect ratio 16:9, landscape. No text, no symbols, no icons — purely non-representational geometry.
