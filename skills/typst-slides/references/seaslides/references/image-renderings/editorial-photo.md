# Style Anchor: editorial-photo

Documentary/editorial photography with natural light, film grain, and a Fujifilm/Leica aesthetic. Slightly desaturated tones, real-world subjects, intentional framing.

---

## 1. Visual Characteristics

- Natural directional light (window light, golden hour, overcast diffusion) — never studio flash
- Visible film grain at ISO 400-800, subtle chromatic shifts in shadows
- Slightly desaturated palette with muted mid-tones and lifted blacks
- Shallow to medium depth of field; bokeh in background elements
- Documentary framing: subject slightly off-center, environmental context preserved

---

## 2. Best-For Scenarios

- Corporate case studies and annual narratives
- Team culture and workplace storytelling
- Editorial longform and thought-leadership decks
- Documentary-style product or service introductions
- Any deck where real-world authenticity builds trust

---

## 3. Prompt Fragment (paste as opening sentences)

> Documentary editorial photograph shot on medium-format film. Natural directional light from a single large window source, visible fine grain at ISO 400, slightly desaturated Fujifilm Pro 400H color science with lifted blacks and muted mid-tones. Shallow depth of field separates subject from environment.

---

## 4. Color Behavior

- Primary HEX manifests in the dominant subject or environment tone (clothing, wall, furniture)
- Secondary HEX appears in the ambient background or large negative-space area
- Accent HEX emerges in one small detail (a book spine, a lamp glow, a fabric swatch)
- Colors are never saturated to full chroma — the film emulation desaturates by ~20%

---

## 5. Don'ts

- No studio flash or ring-light catchlights
- No fully saturated neon or candy colors (breaks the film palette)
- No composite/collage manipulation — single continuous scene only
- No text overlays, watermarks, or date stamps
- No posed stock-photo body language (forced smiles, thumbs-up)

---

## 6. Example Prompt

> Documentary editorial photograph shot on medium-format film. Natural directional light from a single large window source, visible fine grain at ISO 400, slightly desaturated Fujifilm Pro 400H color science with lifted blacks and muted mid-tones. Shallow depth of field separates subject from environment. A single figure sits at a wooden desk in a sunlit co-working space, reviewing printed charts spread across the surface — the figure is seen from a three-quarter rear angle, shoulders and hands visible but face turned away. The desk occupies the lower two-thirds; the upper third is soft-focus exposed-brick wall with afternoon light raking across it. Dominant tone is warm oak-brown from the desk surface (#5C3D2E), secondary is the soft cream wall wash (#F5F0E8), accent appears as a single teal notebook cover (#2C7A7B) partially visible under the papers. Aspect ratio 16:9, landscape. No text, no labels, no visible signage. Figure is a documentary subject, not stock-posed.
