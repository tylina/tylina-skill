# Style Anchor: 3d-render

Soft isometric or perspective 3D with studio lighting, smooth matte surfaces, and controlled shadow. Objects feel tactile and volumetric without photorealism — closer to a product render than a photograph.

---

## 1. Visual Characteristics

- Smooth matte or semi-gloss surfaces with subtle specular highlights
- Soft ambient shadows (8-15% opacity) grounding objects on a surface plane
- Studio-style three-point lighting with one dominant key light
- Isometric or gentle perspective (15-30 degree camera tilt)
- Rounded edges and chamfers on geometric forms — nothing razor-sharp

---

## 2. Best-For Scenarios

- Product showcase and feature highlight decks
- Architecture and spatial design presentations
- Technical system and infrastructure diagrams
- Data visualization with dimensional bar/column charts
- Any deck where objects need to feel tangible and three-dimensional

---

## 3. Prompt Fragment (paste as opening sentences)

> Soft 3D render with smooth matte surfaces and studio three-point lighting. Gentle isometric perspective, rounded edges on all forms, subtle ambient shadows at 10% opacity grounding objects on a neutral surface plane. Surfaces are semi-gloss with one soft specular highlight per object.

---

## 4. Color Behavior

- Primary HEX fills the dominant 3D object (largest volume in scene)
- Secondary HEX defines the ground plane or backdrop surface
- Accent HEX appears on one small object or as a rim-light tint on edges
- Colors appear slightly muted by the lighting model — shadows desaturate, highlights wash toward white

---

## 5. Don'ts

- No photorealistic material textures (wood grain, fabric weave, metal brushing)
- No hard cast shadows with sharp edges (keep shadows soft and diffused)
- No wireframe or polygon visibility — surfaces must be fully resolved
- No extreme perspective distortion or wide-angle lens effects
- No floating objects without a ground reference (everything needs spatial context)

---

## 6. Example Prompt

> Soft 3D render with smooth matte surfaces and studio three-point lighting. Gentle isometric perspective, rounded edges on all forms, subtle ambient shadows at 10% opacity grounding objects on a neutral surface plane. Surfaces are semi-gloss with one soft specular highlight per object. A cluster of three abstract buildings (simplified rectangular prisms with rounded vertical edges) arranged on a light platform — the tallest building is centered and occupies 40% of frame height, two shorter buildings flank it at slight angles. Each building has a different height but identical width, creating a skyline rhythm. The platform is a thin rounded disc sitting on an infinite soft-grey background. Buildings are rendered in deep slate blue (#1E293B), the platform in warm stone (#D6D3D1), and one narrow accent stripe runs horizontally across the tallest building in coral (#FB7185). Aspect ratio 16:9, landscape. No text, no labels, no signage on buildings. Lighting falls from upper-left at 45 degrees.
